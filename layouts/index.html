The roadmap

An academic journey,
milestone by milestone.

From a first-class, Gold-Medal foundation to doctoral research and a forthcoming book — the path that shaped a scholar of digital media.

2018

A Gold-Medal foundation in media & communication

CCS University · Bhartiya Vidya Bhawan · Fairfield (GGSIPU)

A Gold-Medal master's, a distinction-grade bachelor's, and a top-ranked diploma in radio & TV production — theory and hands-on media craft in equal measure.

2017 – 25

Eight years teaching across four universities

Fairfield · KCC (GGSIPU) · AAFT · Amity University

Assistant Professor of Journalism & Mass Communication — teaching communication, advertising, media production and research, coordinating programmes, and mentoring students' field campaigns.

2024

Doctorate: the credibility of fake viral videos

Amity University · PhD, Journalism & Mass Communication

A study of how manipulated viral video shapes belief and trust online — 476 respondents across three generations, interviews with nine media professionals and educators, and cases from India and Britain.

2025

An MSc in the UK & the Prism column

Manchester Metropolitan University · The Present Minds

An international marketing-management lens in Manchester, alongside Prism — translating media-literacy research into essays for a wider public.

Now

Manipulative Realities

Fake viral videos and deepfakes have broken the link between seeing and believing. On platforms with no gatekeeper, the same engine of manipulation quietly reshapes entertainment, religion, and politics. The book is built on original doctoral research across three generations, verified cases from India and Britain, and interviews with media professionals.
