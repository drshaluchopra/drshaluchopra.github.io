About

Bridging media scholarship and marketing insight.

Dr. Shalu Chopra is a journalism and mass communication scholar with a PhD examining the impact and credibility of fake viral videos on social media, and a Gold Medalist master's in the same field.

Over six years she has taught bachelor's and master's students across four institutions — subjects spanning communication, development communication, advertising and media research. She is now pursuing an MSc in International Marketing Management in the UK, and writes the column "Prism" for The Present Minds.
